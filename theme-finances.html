Hospital finances

Are California hospitals making money, and which ones are struggling?

Operating margins from every hospital's filings with the state since 2012: how many lose money, how results differ by ownership and safety-net status, who pays the bills, and the shift to outpatient care.

Key numbers

56%56% of California general-acute hospitals lost money on operations in FY2023.−1.5%Median operating margin was −1.5% in FY2023 and +0.6% in preliminary FY2024.−3.1%Safety-net hospitals had a median FY2023 operating margin of −3.1%, against −0.1% at other general-acute hospitals.
