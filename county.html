County profiles

Hospitals, their finances and prices, and access to care in each of California's 58 counties, next to the state or another county.

How these numbers are built

  • Hospitals are general-acute hospitals that filed an annual financial disclosure with HCAI for fiscal year 2023 or 2024, placed in the county HCAI lists. Beds are licensed beds; residents are 2020 Census counts.
  • Medians are the middle hospital, shown only when at least 3 hospitals have data. Margins and free care are FY2023; payer mix is each payer's share of gross charges.
  • Prices are what commercial insurers paid per case-mix adjusted stay, and that payment as a multiple of what Medicare paid the same hospital (HCAI Hospital Measurement dataset, 2023). Hospitals with fewer than 100 commercial stays are left out.
  • Birth units stopped counts hospitals whose natural births fell to zero and stayed there (this site's count from state filings). Closures are closures or ends of inpatient care since 2012, each checked against news and state records.
  • Districts are the congressional districts the county's residents live in, with the share of the county in each and Urban Institute's benchmark Marketplace premium for a 40-year-old (2026).
