Center brief

The Outpatient Shift: Outpatient Care's Rising Share of California Hospital Revenue, 2014–2025

Across a fixed set of 248 general-acute hospitals, outpatient care's share of gross patient revenue rose from about 36% to 44% between 2014 and 2024. Small and rural hospitals now earn two-thirds to three-quarters of revenue from outpatient care.

USC Center for Health Financing, Policy, and Management
