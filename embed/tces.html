54% of Californians lived in families below the true cost of economic security in 2023, against 49% nationally.

Urban Institute, American Affordability Tracker and Measuring the True Cost of Economic Security (ODC-BY 1.0). The threshold is what a family needs to cover essentials and save for emergencies and the future, set higher than the poverty line. Districts range from 33% (Los Angeles & Santa Monica, CA-36) to 73% (Los Angeles & Culver City, CA-37). District names are this site's shorthand for the two places with the most residents in each.
