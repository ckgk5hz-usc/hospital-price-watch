Find a hospital

    How to read a profile

    • Source. Finances, volumes and payer mix come from each hospital's annual financial disclosure to HCAI. A fiscal year is the year the reporting period ends; FY2024 is preliminary. Reports shorter than a full year are left out.
    • Operating margin is income from patient care and other operations, divided by operating revenue. Total margin adds investment and other income. Hospitals in a system can book costs and income at the parent, so one facility's margin may not show the system's position.
    • State median is the middle hospital among general-acute hospitals with data that year (the explorer's default group), so a few outliers do not move it.
    • Payer mix is each payer's share of gross charges, not of payments; charges are list prices that few payers pay in full.
    • Prices are what commercial insurers and Medicare actually paid per case-mix adjusted stay and per adjusted outpatient visit, from HCAI's Hospital Measurement dataset; shown only for hospitals with at least 100 commercial stays or 1,000 visits. Stars are the CMS Overall Hospital Quality Star Rating.
    • Not shown: days of cash on hand. Systems sweep cash to the parent, so a facility's own figure is misleading.

    Every hospital's figures can be downloaded from its profile. Profiles are rebuilt whenever the underlying data are refreshed.
