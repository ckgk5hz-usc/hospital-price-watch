Overview

An independent, research-first site

The Center's vision is to meaningfully contribute to improving health and health care in local communities, California, in the U.S., and internationally.

Established in 1996 and located within the USC Sol Price School of Public Policy, the Center for Health Financing, Policy and Management aims to advance community, organizational and health system innovation and effectiveness, both domestically and internationally, through leading-edge applied healthcare research, analysis, and knowledge dissemination.

The Center's mission focuses on expanding society's knowledge to improve healthcare, health system performance and effective management of health service delivery.

Five values guide the Center's work:

Innovation Relevance Research excellence Collaboration Knowledge dissemination
Led by Glenn Melnick, PhD — Blue Cross of California Chair in Health Care Finance, and Senior Scholar at the USC Leonard D. Schaeffer Center for Health Policy & Economics.

Data

Where every number on this site comes from

H

California HCAI Hospital Data

California's Department of Health Care Access and Information collects an annual financial disclosure from every licensed hospital in the state — income statements, payer mix, utilization, and more. This is the Center's primary data source.

S

Primary sources

Where a finding comes from a published study rather than raw HCAI data, we cite the journal directly — Health Affairs, Health Affairs Scholar, Medical Care Research Review, and others.

Coming next

Interactive HCAI Dataset Explorer

A tool to filter, chart, and map California hospital data directly — by hospital, district, licensed beds, or payer mix, with exportable charts and built-in regression. Two features we're building toward, modeled on real working examples: a California map with every hospital plotted and color-coded by status, and a 50-state small-multiples grid for year-on-year comparisons.
