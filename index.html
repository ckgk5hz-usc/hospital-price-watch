Questions the data answers

All exhibits

  • Are California hospitals making money?

    More than half lost money on operations in FY2022 and FY2023. The median hospital was slightly in the red in FY2023 and roughly break-even in preliminary FY2024.

    See the exhibits

  • Do bigger hospital systems charge more?

    Sutter Health hospitals charged 30% more under all-or-nothing contracts, and system-affiliated hospitals price emergency visits 20% to 30% higher.

    See the evidence

  • Where is the money going?

    Employer family coverage now costs $26,993 a year. A Yale study traces 91% of premium growth to higher health care spending.

    See the national numbers

How we source every number

Hospital-level figures come from California's Hospital Annual Financial Disclosure data. Everything else is a published finding, cited by author, outlet and date. If a method is borrowed, we say whose it is.
