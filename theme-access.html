Closures

Hospitals that closed or ended inpatient care.

Computed from dataUpdated 2026-10-13

Of 59 California hospitals that stopped filing state disclosures, 18 had closed or ended inpatient care; most of the rest changed name or owner.

Hospitals that filed an HCAI annual disclosure between 2012 and 2023 and then stopped, each researched in news, state, county and hospital sources. A hospital that stops filing has often only been sold or relicensed, so the filing gap alone is not a closure.Map closures in the explorer

Emergency care

The drop in emergency visits and whether it lasted.

Published findingUpdated 2022-11-01

Emergency visits at a large sample of U.S. hospitals fell 18% in 2020 versus 2019, and were still 12% down in early 2022.

Melnick, O'Leary, Zaniello & Abrishamian, American Journal of Emergency Medicine, November 2022: real-time ED registration data, 2020 to Q1 2022 versus 2019.See the abstract
