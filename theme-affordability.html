Households under strain

What people do when costs are too high: skip care, carry medical bills, fall short of a secure budget.

1 of 4
Published findingUpdated 2026-02-25

59% of Californians said they or a family member skipped or delayed care because of cost in the past year.

California Health Care Foundation and NORC at the University of Chicago, The 2026 CHCF California Health Policy Survey: 2,552 adults, October 3 to November 10, 2025; margin of error ±3.0 points overall, larger for income groups. Of those who skipped or delayed, 42% said their condition got worse.Read the survey
