Hospital Price Watch brief

Spending

How fast is total health spending growing, and where does the money go?

Data as of 2026-11-02. Companion to the USC Center for Health Financing, Policy, and Management. Every finding links to its chart and source.

Key findings

  • Commercial health spending per member in California rose 7.8% in 2024, more than twice the state's 3.5% yearly target that began in 2025.1
  • Hospitals accounted for 44.5% of California commercial health spending in 2024: 31.5% outpatient and 13.0% inpatient.2
  • Hospitals took 34% of private health insurance spending in 2024, the largest share.3

Growth vs. the target

How fast commercial spending is rising, against the state's new limit, and what premiums did.

  • Commercial health spending per member in California rose 7.8% in 2024, more than twice the state's 3.5% yearly target that began in 2025.1
  • Large-group premium rates in DMHC-regulated plans rose 10.7% in 2024, the most in the decade shown, and 6.5% in 2025.4

Where the money goes

Which services take the largest share of each dollar.

  • Hospitals accounted for 44.5% of California commercial health spending in 2024: 31.5% outpatient and 13.0% inpatient.2
  • Hospitals took 34% of private health insurance spending in 2024, the largest share.3

Regional differences

Spending per person by the state's 19 regions.

  • Commercial spending per member ranged from $9,477 in San Mateo County to $5,831 in the eastern counties in 2024.5

What drives it

Prices against volume, and medical prices against everything else.

  • Higher health care spending drove 91% of premium growth from 2011 to 2024.6
  • In the West, medical care prices rose 36% from 2016 to 2025, against 38% for all consumer prices.7
