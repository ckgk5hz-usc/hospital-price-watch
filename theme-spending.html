Growth vs. the target

How fast commercial spending is rising, against the state's new limit, and what premiums did.

1 of 2
Published findingUpdated 2026-10-07

Commercial health spending per member in California rose 7.8% in 2024, more than twice the state's 3.5% yearly target that began in 2025.

California Department of Health Care Access and Information (HCAI), Office of Health Care Affordability, Health Care Spending Trends in California, 2023-2024 (2026-10-07). Per member per year: $6,984 in 2023, $7,532 in 2024. The target applies from 2025, so 2024 spending was not held to it. Targets: 2025 3.5%, 2026 3.5%, 2027 3.2%, 2028 3.2%, 2029 3.0%.Read the report
