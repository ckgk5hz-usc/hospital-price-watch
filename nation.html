Issue briefs

Short national briefs on hospital prices and affordability.

Issue brief

Hospital Prices Have Risen Much Faster for Private Insurance Than Medicare Since 2019

Private insurance hospital prices rose 30% between April 2019 and April 2026, against 21% for Medicare rates, about 47% faster.

Godwin & Levinson, KFF, May 2026
Issue brief

System-Affiliated Hospitals Associated with Higher Prices

Hospital prices for inpatient and outpatient care, 2012 to 2022. Emergency visit prices ran roughly 20% to 30% higher at system-affiliated hospitals.

Burke & Chang, Health Care Cost Institute, July 2025
