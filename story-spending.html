Spending · Story

Follow a dollar of health spending

Where private health insurance money goes, how fast California's spending is growing, and how much it differs by region.

Skip to the charts

1 of 5

Start with one dollar

Every dollar that private health insurance spends goes somewhere. Here it is as 100 cents, one square each.

2 of 5

Hospitals take the biggest slice

Nationally, hospitals received 34 cents of each private insurance dollar in 2024, more than physicians and clinics (31 cents). Insurers kept 11 cents for overhead and profit.

CMS National Health Expenditure Accounts, Table 4, 2024. Rounded to whole cents.

3 of 5

California looks similar

In California's commercial market, hospital outpatient care alone took 31.5% of spending in 2024 and inpatient care 13.0%: together 44.5 cents of every dollar. Pharmacy took 24.7%.

California Department of Health Care Access and Information (HCAI), Office of Health Care Affordability, Health Care Spending Trends in California, 2023-2024. A different measure from the national one above: California commercial market only.

4 of 5

And it is growing fast

Commercial spending per person in California rose 7.8% in 2024, from $6,984 to $7,532. The state has set a limit of 3.5% a year starting in 2025, so 2024 spending was not held to it, but it shows how far the market has to come down.

Health Care Spending Trends in California, 2023-2024. Target: HCAI Office of Health Care Affordability.

5 of 5

Where you live matters

Spending per person ranged from $9,477 in San Mateo County to $5,831 in the eastern counties. Some of the gap reflects age and health, which these figures do not adjust for.

Health Care Spending Trends in California, 2023-2024, by the office's 19 regions, 2024.
