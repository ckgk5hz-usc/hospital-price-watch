  • The Outpatient Shift: Outpatient Care's Rising Share of California Hospital Revenue, 2014–2025

    Across a fixed set of 248 general-acute hospitals, outpatient care's share of gross patient revenue rose from 35.8% in 2014 to 43.7% in 2024, and to about 45% in early 2025 filings. Rural hospitals now earn 68.5% of revenue from outpatient care, and the smallest hospitals 76.4%.

    Glenn Melnick & Aditya Subramanian, USC Center for Health Financing, Policy, and Management

  • Hospital Prices Have Risen Much Faster for Private Insurance Than Medicare Since 2019

    Private insurance hospital prices rose 30% between April 2019 and April 2026, against 21% for Medicare rates, about 47% faster.

    Godwin & Levinson, KFF

  • System-Affiliated Hospitals Associated with Higher Prices

    Hospital inpatient and outpatient prices, 2012 to 2022. System-affiliated hospitals had higher prices: inpatient prices were highest at system-affiliated nonprofits and outpatient prices at system-affiliated for-profits.

    Kelsey Burke & Jessica Chang, Health Care Cost Institute
